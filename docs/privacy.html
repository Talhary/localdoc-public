Privacy policy

LocalCast Doc Converter · Last updated 2026-10-26

We collect nothing

LocalCast converts files entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting that leaves your machine. Your files are never uploaded.

The only network connection

LocalCast connects to the internet only when you choose to download an optional AI model, and only to github.com. Each download shows its size, source, licence and hash before you confirm it.

Data stored on your computer

A conversion history (file names, formats, sizes and times, never file contents) is kept locally. You can turn it off or clear it in Settings. Settings and presets are stored in the app's own data folder.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your message only to answer it and delete it after the issue is resolved.

Microsoft Store

Purchases are handled by Microsoft. Microsoft's own privacy statement applies to the Store.
