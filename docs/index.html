LocalCast Doc Converter

Offline document, PDF and image converter for Windows. Your files never leave this computer.

What it does

  • Markdown, Word, HTML and text to PDF and to each other
  • Compress, resize and convert images, including HEIC from iPhones
  • Merge, split, compress and rotate PDFs
  • CSV, Excel, JSON and YAML conversions

Why offline matters

No upload, no account, works in airplane mode. LocalCast contacts exactly one host, and only when you choose to download an optional AI model: this project's releases on github.com.

Status: in development. Coming to the Microsoft Store.
